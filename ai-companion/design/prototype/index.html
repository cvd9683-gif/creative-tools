Try this

  1. Hover a row to see what that chat is working on and which step it's at. Click to keep it open; Esc closes.
  2. Watch a working horse: it creeps right the longer the reply takes, and slows down so it never reaches the finish on its own. When the reply is done it runs the rest of the way. Click once anywhere first so the bell can play.
  3. Open a chat that's your turn. Its horse goes back to the gate.
  4. Done with a chat? Press End on its row (it shows on hover, and always when it's your turn). The row sinks and its receipt prints. Then try Find shorter prompts.
  5. Close a chat's tab: its row sinks and the receipt waits in the slot until you print it.
  6. Add a chat and watch its row rise in.
