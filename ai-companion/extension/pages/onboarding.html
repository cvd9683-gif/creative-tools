Derby

One row per chat

Each Claude, ChatGPT or Grok chat you open in Chrome gets a row on the lawn. The horse runs while the AI is working and reaches the finish when it's your turn. A bell rings once, so you can stop checking tabs.

The further the horse, the longer it has been working. It can't tell how close a reply is to done.

What Derby reads

  • Reads the claude.ai, chatgpt.com and grok.com pages you open: whether a reply is being written, how many messages you've sent, your last prompt, and the chat's text.
  • Why: to show which chat needs you, and to estimate tokens on the receipt when you end a chat.
  • Keeps it in this browser only. Clear it any time in Settings.
  • Sends a chat only when you ask for a handoff or shorter prompts, after showing you exactly what will be sent.
  • Never types, clicks or sends anything on those sites, and never sees any other site.

Plan usage isn't readable by extensions, so Derby never shows “tokens left”. Token numbers on receipts are estimates from text length.
