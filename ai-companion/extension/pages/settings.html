Motion follows your computer's “reduce motion” setting: when it's on, horses appear at their spot instead of galloping.

Handoff service

Handoffs and shorter-prompt suggestions use Derby's own service, which calls the Claude API. Chat text is sent only after you check it and press Send.

Your data

Derby keeps your rows, receipts and the chat text it read in this browser only. Clearing removes all of it.
