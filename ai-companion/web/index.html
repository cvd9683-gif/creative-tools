Derby demo · made-up data

One row per AI chat. The horse runs while the AI works and reaches the finish when it's your turn. End a chat to print its receipt.

Try

  1. Hover or tap a row to see what that chat is doing.
  2. Wait for a horse to finish. Click anywhere once first so the bell can ring.
  3. Press End on a row to print its receipt, then Find shorter prompts.
  4. Paste one of your own chats to get a real receipt and handoff.
